Dragon Adventures

v…

Build a little camp, feed a colorful dragon, then explore when you are ready.

Offline-friendly · local saves · no accounts

How to play

  • WASD move · double-tap W sprint · Space jump (or take off when mounted)
  • Click the game to look around. Left click = sword swing. Prompts appear when something is in range.
  • 1 Sword · 2 Drumstick · 3 Bread · 4 Wood block · 5 Stone block
  • Select drumstick, walk up to a dragon, right-click when prompted to feed & tame. Then E mount · Space fly · land low · E dismount.
  • Select Wood/Stone to build: ghost preview shows placement · right-click place · X remove looking-at block. Place a few on the yellow pad, then press R Ready.
  • Monsters stay peaceful until you hit them — only that one fights back.
  • C or Home (or Look button) recenters the camera if it tips up.
  • Esc = pause / save / install tips.

Install / offline

  • Play online once until the HUD shows Offline ready.
  • Bookmark this page, or use your browser’s “Install” / “Add to …” option if offered (labels vary by Chrome version — we don’t invent ChromeOS menu names).
  • Quit the browser fully, turn network off, then open the bookmark — the game should load from cache.
  • ZIP archive (download link in pause) is a static site backup for hosting — it is not a file:// app installer.
  • Export Save makes a JSON backup. Local saves stay on this device for this site address; powerwash or clearing site data can erase them.
