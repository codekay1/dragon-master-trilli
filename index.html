Dragon Master Trilli

Build your house, train a colorful dragon, and explore when you are ready.

Offline-friendly · local saves · no accounts

How to play

  • WASD move · double-tap W sprint · Space jump
  • Click game to look · Left click attack · Right click place / feed
  • 1–7 inventory · E mount · feed a dragon to tame · R ready for battle
  • Monsters stay peaceful until you hit them — only that one fights back.
  • Esc opens pause / save / customize.

Paused

Combat is off while paused. Escape or Resume to continue.

Saves stay on this Chromebook (this site address). Export a backup — powerwash can erase local data. Persistence is requested, not guaranteed. Changing port/origin starts a fresh save.
